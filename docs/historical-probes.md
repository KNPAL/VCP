# Historical response and account probes (KAN-29)

`normalizeHistoricalResponse` consumes daily response tuples and an immutable instrument mapping plus verified exchange calendar. The broker JSON decoder preserves numeric source lexemes before any floating-point conversion. Prices retain six-place exact decimal input; raw volume remains an integer string. Both midnight IST daily labels and the verified session-open label are accepted. Arbitrary intraday labels, malformed tuples, contradictory duplicate bars, invalid OHLC, unverified zero volume, wrong mappings and unknown calendars cannot establish complete coverage.

Every response reports its raw count, accepted unique count, actual span, expected count, missing sessions, rejected reasons and content revision. Exactly 500 rows with missing expected sessions raises a possible truncation flag. Requested days never stand in for received sessions. Live transport remains bounded to 400 calendar days; the probe evaluator also accepts wider captured responses for comparison.

`historicalProbe` compares a wide response with at least two overlapping narrower responses for the same mapped listing. It checks both coverage and per-session content hashes. Authenticated/static-IP evidence and verified account quota observations are recorded separately. Effective ceilings are the lower of verified account limits and V1's 1/second, 60/minute, 3000/hour limits. Missing, expired or future evidence produces UNKNOWN; contradictory or incomplete data produces FAILED. Probe evaluation itself does not issue requests, infer account capacity from empty responses, or raise the live throttle.

`recordHistoricalProbe` stores the evidence and revision under a caller-supplied UUID and non-secret account hash. Identical repeats are idempotent; conflicting UUID reuse fails. PostgreSQL prevents updates and deletions. Raw response bodies, credentials and raw provider errors are excluded. Migration 16 adds this evidence table.

Synthetic tests cover passing comparisons, conflicting overlap, unknown auth/quota/calendar, future evidence, exact numeric decoding, duplicate and malformed rows, and a 740-session response truncated to 500 rows. PostgreSQL tests verify immutable evidence and idempotency. No live account quota or real broker data has been verified. Credential provisioning (KAN-28) and production backfill orchestration (KAN-33) remain separate unfinished work.
